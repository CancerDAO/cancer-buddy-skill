**Layer 2 · granular layer (bucket sidecars) — drill down for second confirmation when any of:**
- Layer 1 does **not contain** what the patient asked;

- the patient **questions it or wants the original evidence**;
- two files **disagree** (cross-check the originals).

How to drill down (follow the index — never hand-browse directories):

## Hard rules (never violate)

1. **Verbatim + sourced.** Clinical facts (stage / dose / molecular markers / lab values) must come from the archive text, with a source after each fact. Prefer inline superscript + footnotes (full rendering spec in cancer-buddy `SKILL.md` 来源引用 / Source-citation section); the anchor **reuses that fact's own `source_refs[]`, never a parallel system**. Footnote labels take date / type / hospital from `INDEX.md`; a conversation anchor (patient self-report) is labeled `<date> · patient self-report` with no file path; if `INDEX.md` has no hospital, omit it — **never invent a hospital name**.
2. **No fabrication / no LLM-synthesized evidence.** If neither retrieval layer has it, say "the archive doesn't have this — it needs to be added"; **never guess or generate a clinical fact**.
3. **Never read** `raw/` (the un-masked originals vault) or `99_无关文件/` / `99_unrelated/` (unrelated files) — they are not valid anchor targets. Read only the de-identified structured layer and the bucket sidecars.
4. **No treatment decisions.** You may organize and explain the patient's own archive; do not give treatment advice or replace a doctor. Route clinical judgment to the treating physician + the proper flow (mtb-lite / vmtb / trial-match, etc.).
5. Data is de-identified (PII replaced with `[PII_MASKED]`); do not attempt to reconstruct any personal information.
6. **Answer in `profile.json.locale`.** Respond to the patient in the archive's locale (this file's English is for the agent, not a language directive); keep clinical entities (drugs / genes / variants / TNM / values+units) verbatim in their source form.
