# Founder Story Boundary

This file is non-clinical brand governance. It must not be loaded into patient-facing or clinical
workflows.

A public founder story may illustrate that organized records, questions, and multidisciplinary care can
help patients navigate complex systems. A single person's course cannot establish diagnostic strategy,
treatment efficacy, treatment attribution, cost, access time, or a reproducible care pathway.

## Prohibited translations into product behavior

- Do not encourage “every available test,” repeated testing without indication, or broad multi-omics.
- Do not encourage multiple experimental treatments in parallel.
- Do not infer efficacy from immune-cell, ctDNA, biomarker, imaging, or single-cell changes.
- Do not claim that FFPE universally destroys nucleic acids or that fresh-frozen tissue is always required.
- Do not present expanded access, cross-border therapy, an IND, or an experimental intervention as a
  treatment shortcut.
- Do not claim that Cancer Buddy replaces an oncologist, pathologist, molecular tumor board,
  bioinformatician, pharmacist, legal team, or care coordinator.

If the story is used in brand material, cite each factual claim, disclose `n=1`, separate concurrent
interventions, unknown attribution, selection bias, cost/access inequity, and current outcome status.

Cancer Buddy's clinical role is limited to source-preserving organization, evidence retrieval, question
preparation, and navigation under `references/clinical-content-governance.md`.
